Privacy Policy for Banana Blast

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Banana Blast (bananablast.se). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data you share with us.

1. Data We Collect

When you play Banana Blast and submit your score to the global leaderboard, we only store the nickname you choose along with your game score. We do not collect real names, email addresses, or passwords.

2. How We Use Data

The nickname and score are used solely to display the public top leaderboard.

3. Third-Party Services (Ads)

We may use third-party advertising networks to display ads. These networks may use cookies or device identifiers to serve relevant ads. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information on how they handle data.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us via contact@bananablast.se.
